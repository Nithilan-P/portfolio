CampusCare

In progress, 70% built

Campus complaints are often raised on paper or lost in messages, so students never know who is handling them. CampusCare lets students report an issue in a few taps and follow it to resolution, while staff assign and resolve everything from one dashboard.

Each complaint moves through clear stages, from raised to resolved, so students always see where things stand and staff can prioritise what needs attention first. The goal is simple: fewer ignored issues and a campus that responds faster.

Built with React, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, and JWT authentication.

SplitEasy

Completed

Roommates sharing bills often forget payments and end up in awkward settle-ups. SplitEasy records each shared expense, splits it across the group, and keeps running balances so everyone sees exactly who owes whom.

Expenses can be added in seconds and divided equally or by custom amounts. The app keeps every balance up to date and suggests the simplest way to settle, so no one has to do the arithmetic or chase anyone for money.

Built with React, TypeScript, Express.js, and PostgreSQL.

TaskFlow

Completed

Small teams lose track of tasks across chats and notes. TaskFlow is a drag-and-drop kanban board where each task is a card that moves across columns, giving the whole team one shared view of the work.

Teams create boards, add columns that match their workflow, and drag cards forward as work progresses. Every card carries its details, so nothing important is buried in a conversation and everyone knows what comes next.

Built with React, TypeScript, Node.js, and MongoDB.

DevLink

Completed

Developers have work spread over many platforms but no single tidy page to share. DevLink lets them pick a theme, add their links, and publish one clean URL that only they can edit.

Each page is built from a chosen theme and a list of links that can be added, reordered, or removed at any time. Authorization restricts editing to the owner, so the page stays accurate and trustworthy.

Built with React, Express.js, MySQL, and JWT authentication.
